**Edge sharpening (2026-08-30, cont.):** gradient-matching edge losses fail categorically (noisy guide gradients poison the match: span targets collapse dark cross-pol, per-channel 2-look targets re-inject speckle) — `edge_sharp_lambda` is a documented negative, keep 0. The working tool is `edge_boost=1.0`: edge-masked unsharp of the FINAL output (mask = multi-look span log-gradients + 0.3x phase snr-coherence gradients; `edge_boost_dark=0.2` per-channel dark gate is mandatory or real ENLr(HV) collapses). GT: PSNR(HH)+0.18dB, EPI up in both channels, ENL unchanged. Full recommended stack now: MERLIN + PH(b3,f0.5) + whiteness(0.05; lags 3-5 real) + polish(0.5) + edge_boost(1.0).

**Key findings (for the thesis):**
- amp² is a sufficient statistic for reflectivity: RI decomposition adds supervision density (100% of pixels vs ~30% masked), not information; single-channel phase is uniform (no per-pixel info). Cross-channel phase (HH–VV CPD) DOES carry scattering-type signal (concentration 0.26 on structures vs 0.09 distributed) but the uint8 phase files are folded to [0,π] and inconsistent with re/im — too degraded to exploit; needs signed float SLC.
- Noisy-reference EPI/SSIM are misleading: vs the KNOWN clean, EPI rises 0.65→0.81+. Fair protocol = ratio-image ENL + synthetic-GT (scripts in scratchpad were used; results under results/ri_compare/, gitignored).
- `merlin_loss="nll"` collapses dark cross-pol channels to zero (black HV) — documented, default stays "l1".
- MERLIN output sits on the median convention (~13% darker channel means); corr/ENL/EPI are scale-invariant, MAD/RMSE are not — report scale-normalized MAD.

**Default-off phase knobs — settled (2026-08-31):** `phase_protect` and `phase_surface_boost` both stay 0, now with evidence. Key methodological point: the trainer does `tv_weights *= phase_factor` WITHOUT renormalizing, so any knob that shifts the factor's mean is partly just a global `tv_mult` change (only `nl_w` is mean-1, i.e. shape-only) — every phase-knob claim therefore needs a MEAN-MATCHED tv_mult control. `phase_protect`: its `det` map is near estimator noise (independent cross-channel agreement +0.06..+0.09 vs +0.02 null; the +0.71 HV-VH agreement is reciprocity = same channel, not evidence), and p=0.6 is matched or beaten by the tv_mult=7.9 control on every metric. `phase_surface_boost`: s=1.0 gives ENL-ROI +1.7/+2.4% but the tv_mult=11.3 control reproduces it, and in the high-`surface` ROIs where it should act it shows no advantage (ENLsurf 1.1376 vs ctrl 1.1393). The `surface` map is still a valid scene descriptor (ENL ~1.9x higher in its high ROIs, corr −0.21 with local CV).
