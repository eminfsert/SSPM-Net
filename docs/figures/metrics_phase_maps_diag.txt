Reading: 'snr' is a strong, verified map (0.52 correlation with brightness
vs 0.00 for the null).  'surface' is weak but physically consistent -- higher
on homogeneous low-CV areas (0.438 vs 0.260) and anti-correlated with local
CV -- so it does track distributed/surface scattering, though most of its
variance is estimator noise.  'det' is close to pure estimator noise: the
independent cross-channel agreement is +0.06..+0.09 versus +0.02 for the
null, and it marks point-like targets only faintly (0.405 vs 0.347 on the
strongest 0.1% peak-to-local-mean pixels, corr +0.048 overall).
